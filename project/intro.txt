========================================
             MYSTERY ROOM
          ESCAPE THE HOUSE
========================================

You wake up in a strange house.

You do not remember how you got there.

You are trapped inside.

You start in the Bedroom.

From there, you discover three possible
ways to escape:

        KEY
         |
    MAIN DOOR
         |
        END

        BOOK
         |
      PUZZLE
         |
        END

        COIN
         |
 SECRET PASSAGE
         |
        END

Each route requires a different item.

Explore the house carefully.
Find an item.
Choose your route.
Use the correct item to escape.

You can also play a number guessing game
to earn extra points.

Your objective:

ESCAPE THE HOUSE.

Good luck!
